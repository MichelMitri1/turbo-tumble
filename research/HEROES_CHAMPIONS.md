# Heroes and Champions

Champions in the live hand-card roster: Mighty Miner, Skeleton King, Archer Queen, Golden Knight, Monk, Little Prince, Goblinstein, Boss Bandit.

Tracked Hero forms: Mini P.E.K.K.A, Musketeer, Wizard, Goblins, Ice Golem, Mega Minion, Barbarian Barrel, Magic Archer, Balloon, Bowler, Tombstone, Valkyrie. The March 2026 update changed deck construction to one Evolution, one Hero, and one Wild slot. Hero Balloon’s Coffin Cadets launches Skeletons toward targets; Hero Magic Archer’s Triple Threat backsteps, leaves a decoy, and empowers the next shot; Hero Barbarian Barrel’s Rowdy Reroll sends it down the lane again.

The battle engine tags ability-bearing cards and implements their passive defining families. Dedicated user-triggered cooldown buttons for every Champion/Hero are not yet complete.
