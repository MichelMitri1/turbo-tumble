# Battle rules

- Eight-card decks, four cards in hand, one visible next card.
- Maximum 10 elixir; continuous regeneration.
- Three-minute regulation: normal elixir for the first two minutes, double elixir in the final minute.
- Two-minute sudden-death window uses triple elixir in this normalized mode; lowest remaining tower health resolves the time limit.
- A side tower awards one crown. Destroying the Crown Keep ends the match and brings the winner to three crowns.
- Crown Keeps begin inactive and activate when damaged; side Guard Towers begin active.
- Troops/buildings deploy only in owned territory. Spells and burrowing deployment can target either side.
- Ground movement crosses one of two bridges; air crosses the river directly.
- Simulation runs at 30 deterministic ticks per second, independent of rendering FPS.
