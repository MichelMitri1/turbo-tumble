# Engine recordings

The engine loops in this folder are cut, pitch-flattened and loop-crossfaded from
recordings on Wikimedia Commons by `tools/make-engine-loops.mjs`. They are adaptations
licensed under the same licences as their sources (**CC BY-SA**). The rest of the game is
not affected by these licences.
